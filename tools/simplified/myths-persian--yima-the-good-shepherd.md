---
title: Yima, the Good Shepherd
adaptedFrom: darmesteter-avesta--yima-the-good-shepherd
order: 2
namesNote: From the Vendidad, one of the holy books of the Zoroastrians. Ahura Mazda is the Wise Lord whom Zoroastrians worship, and Zarathushtra (Zoroaster) was his prophet. Spenta Armaiti is the holy spirit of the earth. Yima is the king that the Shahnameh calls Jamshid.
---

Zarathushtra asked Ahura Mazda, "O Ahura Mazda, most generous Spirit, Maker of the world, Holy One! Who was the first person, before me, that you spoke with and taught your Religion?"

Ahura Mazda answered, "It was fair Yima, the good shepherd, O holy Zarathushtra! He was the first person, before you, that I spoke with and taught my Religion. I said to him, 'Fair Yima, son of Vivanghat, be the teacher and the bearer of my Religion!' But fair Yima answered me, 'I was not born for that, and I was not taught to be the teacher and the bearer of your Religion.'

"Then I, Ahura Mazda, said to him, 'If you will not be the teacher and the bearer of my Religion, then make my world grow. Feed my world, and rule it, and watch over it.'

"And fair Yima answered, 'Yes! I will make your world grow. Yes! I will feed your world, and rule it, and watch over it. While I am king, there will be no cold wind and no hot wind, no sickness and no death.'

"Then I, Ahura Mazda, brought him two things: a golden seal, and a dagger trimmed with gold. And so Yima ruled.

"Under Yima's rule, three hundred winters went by, and the earth filled up with flocks and herds, with people and dogs and birds, and with red blazing fires, until there was no more room for flocks and herds and people. Then I warned fair Yima, 'Fair Yima, son of Vivanghat, the earth is full of flocks and herds, of people and dogs and birds and red blazing fires, and there is no more room for flocks and herds and people.'

"Then Yima stepped forward, in the light, southwards, along the path of the sun. He pressed the earth with the golden seal, and pierced it with the dagger, and said, 'O Spenta Armaiti, kindly open up, and stretch yourself far and wide, to carry flocks and herds and people.' And Yima made the earth bigger by one third than it was before. And flocks and herds and people came to it, as many as he wished.

"Under Yima's rule, six hundred winters went by, and once again the earth was full of flocks and herds, of people and dogs and birds and red blazing fires, and there was no more room. And I warned fair Yima again. Then Yima stepped forward, in the light, southwards, along the path of the sun. He pressed the earth with the golden seal, and pierced it with the dagger, and said, 'O Spenta Armaiti, kindly open up, and stretch yourself far and wide, to carry flocks and herds and people.' And Yima made the earth bigger by two thirds than it was before. And flocks and herds and people came to it, as many as he wished.

"Under Yima's rule, nine hundred winters went by, and once again the earth was full, and there was no more room. And I warned fair Yima again. Then Yima stepped forward, in the light, southwards, along the path of the sun. He pressed the earth with the golden seal, and pierced it with the dagger, and said, 'O Spenta Armaiti, kindly open up, and stretch yourself far and wide, to carry flocks and herds and people.' And Yima made the earth bigger by three thirds than it was before. And flocks and herds and people came to it, as many as he wished."

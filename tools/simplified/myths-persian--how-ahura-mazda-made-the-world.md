---
title: How Ahura Mazda Made the World
basedOn: the Bundahishn, the Zoroastrian book of the creation, written down in Middle Persian about the 800s AD
workId: persian-creation
order: 1
livingTradition: Zoroastrians
namesNote: The Bundahishn calls the two spirits Ohrmazd and Ahriman. Gayomard is the Kayumars of the Shahnameh, the first king.
---

In the beginning there were two spirits. High above, in endless light, was Ahura Mazda, the Wise Lord, who knows everything and is wholly good. Far below, in the deep darkness, was Angra Mainyu, the Evil Spirit, who is slow to understand and wants only to destroy. Between the light and the darkness there was an empty space.

Ahura Mazda knew that Angra Mainyu was there, and that one day he would attack. But Angra Mainyu did not know about Ahura Mazda, until he rose up out of the darkness and saw the light. Then he was filled with envy, and he rushed at the light to destroy it. But when he saw how strong and great Ahura Mazda was, he fled back into the darkness, and there he made many demons to help him.

Ahura Mazda offered him peace. "Help my creatures, and praise them," he said, "and you will never grow old or die." But Angra Mainyu would not. "I will destroy your creatures for ever," he said, "and turn them against you."

So Ahura Mazda said, "Then let us set a time for our battle: nine thousand years." Angra Mainyu agreed, for he did not know how it would end. But Ahura Mazda knew. Then Ahura Mazda said the holy prayer, the Ahunavar, and in it he showed Angra Mainyu that at the end of time he would be beaten and destroyed. Angra Mainyu was so stunned that he fell back into the darkness, and lay there, unable to move, for three thousand years.

While he lay there, Ahura Mazda made the world. First he made the sky, bright and strong. Second, he made the water. Third, he made the earth, round and firm. Fourth, he made the plants. Fifth, he made the first animal, a great white bull, shining like the moon. And sixth, he made the first man, Gayomard, shining like the sun. And through it all there was fire. Everything was good, and there was no pain, and no death.

But when the three thousand years were over, Angra Mainyu woke up, and he burst into the world. He broke through the sky, and spoiled the water, and bored a hole through the earth. He brought darkness, and poison, and pain, and death. The plants withered, and the great white bull died, and after thirty years Gayomard died too.

But Angra Mainyu could not destroy the good creation. From the bull came all the animals in the world. And from Gayomard, after forty years, a rhubarb plant grew out of the earth, and from it came the first man and woman, Mashya and Mashyana. And from them come all the people in the world.

Ever since then, good and evil have been mixed together in the world. But at the end of time, Angra Mainyu will be beaten for ever, and the world will be made fresh and new.

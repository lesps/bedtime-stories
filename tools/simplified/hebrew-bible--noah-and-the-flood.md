The Lord saw that the people on the earth had become very wicked. Everything they thought about was bad, all the time. The earth was full of violence. And the Lord was sorry that He had made people on the earth, and it grieved Him in His heart.

"I will wipe them all off the face of the earth," said the Lord.

But Noah found favour in the eyes of the Lord. Noah was a good man, who did what was right, and Noah walked with God. He had three sons: Shem, Ham and Japheth.

God said to Noah, "I am going to bring a great flood of water over the earth, and everything on the earth will die. So make yourself an ark of gopher wood. Make rooms in it, and cover it with pitch inside and outside, to keep the water out. Make it three hundred cubits long, fifty cubits wide and thirty cubits high. Give it a window, and put a door in its side, and make it with three floors: lower, second and third.

"But I will make My covenant with you, a promise. You shall go into the ark: you, and your sons, and your wife, and your sons' wives. And bring into the ark two of every kind of living creature, a male and a female, to keep them alive with you: birds of every kind, and animals of every kind, and every creeping thing. And take every kind of food that is eaten, and store it away, as food for you and for them."

And Noah did everything that God told him to do.

Then the Lord said to Noah, "Go into the ark, you and all your family, for I have seen that you are good. Take with you seven pairs of every clean animal, and one pair of the animals that are not clean, and seven pairs of the birds, to keep every kind alive on the earth. For in seven days I will make it rain on the earth for forty days and forty nights."

So Noah went into the ark with his sons, and his wife, and his sons' wives. And the animals came to Noah two by two, male and female, and went into the ark, just as God had told him. And the Lord shut him in.

After the seven days, the waters of the flood came upon the earth. All the fountains of the great deep burst open, and the windows of heaven were opened, and the rain fell on the earth for forty days and forty nights.

The waters rose and rose. They lifted up the ark, and it floated on the face of the waters. The waters rose so high that they covered all the high mountains under the whole sky. And every living thing that moved on the earth died: the birds, and the cattle, and the wild animals, and all the people. Only Noah was left, and those who were with him in the ark. And the waters covered the earth for a hundred and fifty days.

But God remembered Noah, and all the animals with him in the ark. He made a wind blow over the earth, and the waters began to go down. The fountains of the deep and the windows of heaven were closed, and the rain stopped. Slowly the waters went down. And in the seventh month, the ark came to rest on the mountains of Ararat. The waters went on going down, until the tops of the mountains could be seen.

After forty days, Noah opened the window he had made in the ark. He sent out a raven, and it flew to and fro until the waters had dried up from the earth.

Then he sent out a dove, to see if the waters had gone down. But the dove found nowhere to rest her foot, because the water still covered the whole earth. So she came back to the ark, and Noah reached out his hand and took her, and brought her in.

He waited another seven days, and sent out the dove again. And in the evening the dove came back to him, and there in her beak was a fresh olive leaf! So Noah knew that the waters had gone down from the earth.

He waited another seven days, and sent out the dove once more. And this time she did not come back.

Noah took the cover off the ark and looked out, and the ground was drying. And when the earth was dry, God said to Noah, "Come out of the ark: you, and your wife, and your sons, and your sons' wives. And bring out every living creature that is with you, so that they can spread out over the earth, and have young, and fill it."

So Noah came out, with his family, and all the animals and the birds came out of the ark, family by family.

Noah built an altar to the Lord, and made an offering on it. And the Lord said in His heart, "I will never again curse the ground because of people, and I will never again destroy every living thing, as I have done. As long as the earth lasts, seedtime and harvest, cold and heat, summer and winter, day and night, shall never stop."

God blessed Noah and his sons, and said to them, "Have many children, and fill the earth.

"Now I am making My covenant with you, and with your children after you, and with every living creature that came out of the ark with you: never again will all living things be destroyed by the waters of a flood. Never again will there be a flood to destroy the earth.

"And this is the sign of My promise, between Me and you and every living creature, for ever: I have set My bow in the clouds. Whenever I bring clouds over the earth, and the rainbow is seen in the clouds, I will remember My promise, and the waters shall never again become a flood to destroy all living things. When the rainbow is in the clouds, I will look at it, and remember the promise that lasts for ever, between God and every living creature on the earth."

The Pharisees and the teachers of the law grumbled because Jesus welcomed sinners and ate with them. So Jesus told them this story.

There was once a man who had two sons. The younger son said to his father, "Father, give me my share of everything you own." So the father divided what he had between his sons.

Not many days later, the younger son packed up everything he had, and went far away to another country. And there he wasted all his money on wild living.

When he had spent it all, a terrible famine came to that country, and he began to go hungry. So he went to work for a man who lived there, and the man sent him into his fields to feed the pigs. He was so hungry that he wished he could fill himself up with the pods that the pigs were eating. But no one gave him anything.

At last he came to his senses. "All my father's hired servants have more bread than they can eat," he said, "and here I am, dying of hunger! I will get up and go to my father, and I will say to him, 'Father, I have sinned against heaven, and against you. I am not good enough to be called your son any more. Let me be one of your hired servants.'"

So he got up and went to his father. But while he was still a long way off, his father saw him, and felt sorry for him. He ran to his son, and threw his arms around him, and kissed him.

"Father," said the son, "I have sinned against heaven, and against you. I am not good enough to be called your son any more."

But the father said to his servants, "Quick! Bring out the best robe and put it on him. Put a ring on his hand, and shoes on his feet. And bring the calf we have been fattening, and let us eat and celebrate! For this son of mine was dead, and is alive again. He was lost, and is found." And they began to celebrate.

Now the elder son was out in the field. As he came near the house, he heard music and dancing. He called one of the servants and asked what was going on.

"Your brother has come home," said the servant, "and your father has made a feast of the fattened calf, because he has got him back safe and well."

But the elder son was angry, and would not go in. So his father came out and begged him to come in.

"Look!" said the elder son. "All these years I have worked for you, and I have never once disobeyed you. And you never gave me even a young goat, so that I could have a party with my friends. But when this son of yours comes home, after wasting all your money, you make a feast of the fattened calf for him!"

"My son," said the father, "you are always with me, and everything I have is yours. But we had to celebrate and be glad. For this brother of yours was dead, and is alive again. He was lost, and is found."

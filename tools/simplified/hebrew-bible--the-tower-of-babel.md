Long ago, the whole earth had one language, and everyone spoke with the same words.

As people travelled east, they found a wide plain in the land of Shinar, and they settled there.

They said to each other, "Come, let us make bricks, and bake them hard in the fire." So they used bricks instead of stone, and tar instead of mortar.

Then they said, "Come, let us build ourselves a city, and a tower with its top in the heavens. Let us make a name for ourselves, so that we are not scattered all over the face of the earth."

And the Lord came down to see the city and the tower that the people were building.

The Lord said, "Look, they are one people, and they all have one language. And this is only the beginning of what they will do. Nothing they plan to do will be too hard for them. Come, let Us go down and mix up their language, so that they cannot understand each other."

So the Lord scattered them from there over the face of the whole earth, and they stopped building the city.

That is why the city was called Babel: because there the Lord mixed up the language of the whole earth, and from there the Lord scattered people all over the face of the earth.

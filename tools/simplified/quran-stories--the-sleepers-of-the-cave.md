---
title: The Sleepers of the Cave
adaptedFrom: pickthall-koran--the-people-of-the-cave
order: 1
namesNote: From the eighteenth chapter of the Qur'an, called The Cave. Allah is the Arabic word for God. The Qur'an does not say what the young men's names were, or how many of them there were.
---

Long ago, there were some young men who believed in their Lord, and Allah gave them more and more guidance. Allah made their hearts strong, and they stood up and said, "Our Lord is the Lord of the heavens and the earth. We will never pray to any god but Him, for that would be a terrible wrong. Our people have chosen other gods instead of Him, but they have no clear proof for them. And who does more wrong than someone who tells a lie about Allah?"

So the young men left their people, and the gods their people worshipped, and they went to shelter in a cave. "Our Lord will spread His mercy over us," they said, "and give us a place to rest in our trouble."

As they went to the cave, they prayed, "Our Lord! Give us mercy from You, and show us the right thing to do in our trouble."

Then Allah closed their ears, and they slept in the cave for many years.

When the sun rose, it moved away from their cave to the right, and when it set, it passed them by on the left, while they lay in the wide space inside. That was one of the signs of Allah.

Anyone who looked at them would have thought they were awake, but they were asleep. Allah turned them over, to the right and to the left. And their dog lay at the doorway, with his paws stretched out. Anyone who saw them would have turned and run away, filled with awe.

Then, after a long time, Allah woke them up, so that they would ask each other questions.

"How long have we been here?" said one of them.

"A day," they said, "or part of a day."

Others said, "Our Lord knows best how long we have been here. Now let's send one of us to the city with this silver coin of ours. Let him see where the purest food is, and bring us some. He must be polite, and he must not let anyone know about us. For if the people find out about us, they will throw stones at us, or make us go back to their religion, and then we will never do well."

Then Allah let the people of the city find out about the young men, so that they would know that Allah's promise is true, and that the Last Day will surely come.

The people of the city argued about the young men. Some said, "Put up a building over them. Their Lord knows best about them." And the ones who won the argument said, "We will build a place of worship over them."

Some people say there were three young men, and their dog made four. Others say there were five, and their dog made six. They are only guessing. And others say there were seven, and their dog made eight. But Allah knows best how many there were.

Never say about anything, "I will do that tomorrow," without saying, "if Allah wills." And if you forget, remember your Lord.

It is said that they stayed in their cave for three hundred years, and nine more. But Allah knows best how long they stayed. He knows everything that is hidden in the heavens and the earth.

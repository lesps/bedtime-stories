---
title: Brahma on the Lotus
adaptedFrom: ganguli-mahabharata--the-lotus-from-vishnus-navel

namesNote: From the Mahabharata, a holy epic of the Hindus. Vishnu is the god who keeps the world safe, and Brahma is the creator. Sesha is the great serpent on whom Vishnu sleeps.
---

Long ago, the whole world had come to an end, and everything was one wide sea of water. Then Vishnu, the Lord of the universe, who never grows old, lay asleep on the great hood of the serpent Sesha, whose body circled the whole earth. And as he slept, a lotus, as beautiful and bright as the sun, grew up out of his navel. Out of the lotus came Brahma, the grandfather of all the worlds, with four faces.

Vishnu lay stretched out on the serpent's hood, wearing a crown and a shining jewel and robes of purple silk, and he shone like a thousand suns.

After a while, two mighty demons named Madhu and Kaitabha saw Vishnu lying there, and Brahma sitting on the lotus. They began to frighten Brahma, and Brahma trembled on his seat. When Brahma trembled, the stalk of the lotus trembled too, and when the lotus stalk trembled, Vishnu woke up.

When Vishnu saw the two mighty demons, he said, "Welcome, mighty ones! I am pleased with you, so I will give you whatever you wish."

But the proud demons laughed. "No, you ask us for a wish, O God!" they said. "We will grant it. Ask us for anything you like."

"Very well," said Vishnu. "There is one thing I want. You are both so mighty that there is no one like you. So let me put an end to you. That is what I want, for the good of the world."

"We have never told a lie," said Madhu and Kaitabha, "not even as a joke. No one can win against Time, so do what you have said. But we ask one thing of you. You must do it in a place that is not covered by water. And we wish to be born again as your sons. Do not break your promise to us."

"Yes," said Vishnu. "It will be as you wish."

Then Vishnu looked for a place that was not covered by water. But the whole earth and sky were covered, and he could not find one anywhere. Then he saw that his own thighs were not covered at all. And there, on his thighs, he struck down Madhu and Kaitabha with his sharp discus.

When Solomon was a young king, the Lord appeared to him in a dream one night, at Gibeon. And God said, "Ask for whatever you want Me to give you."

Solomon said, "You were very kind to my father David, because he walked before You in truth and goodness. And You have kept on being kind to him, by giving him a son to sit on his throne. Now, O Lord my God, You have made me king in my father's place. But I am only a little child. I don't know how to lead. And I am in the middle of Your people, a great people, too many to count. So give me an understanding heart, to judge Your people, and to know the difference between good and evil. For who could judge this great people of Yours?"

The Lord was pleased that Solomon had asked for this. And God said to him, "Because you have asked for this, and not for a long life for yourself, or riches, or the lives of your enemies, but for understanding, to know what is right, I will do as you ask. I will give you a wise and understanding heart, so that there has never been anyone like you before, and there never will be after. And I will also give you what you did not ask for, both riches and honour, so that no other king will be like you all your days. And if you walk in My ways, as your father David did, I will give you a long life."

Then Solomon woke up, and knew it had been a dream. He went to Jerusalem, and stood before the ark of the covenant of the Lord, and made offerings, and gave a feast for all his servants.

Some time later, two women came to the king and stood before him.

"Oh, my lord," said the first woman, "this woman and I live in the same house. I had a baby while she was there. Three days later, she had a baby too. We were alone; there was no one else in the house but the two of us. In the night, this woman's baby died, because she lay on top of him in her sleep. And she got up in the middle of the night, while I was asleep, and took my son from beside me, and laid her dead baby beside me instead. When I got up in the morning to feed my baby, he was dead! But when I looked at him closely in the light, I saw that he was not my son at all."

"No!" said the other woman. "The living child is my son, and the dead one is yours!"

"No!" said the first woman. "The dead one is yours, and the living one is mine!" And so they argued in front of the king.

The king said, "This one says, 'My son is the living one, and yours is dead.' And that one says, 'No, your son is dead, and mine is the living one.'" Then the king said, "Bring me a sword." And they brought a sword to the king.

"Cut the living child in two," said the king, "and give half to one woman and half to the other."

Then the woman whose son was really the living child cried out, for her heart was full of love for her son. "Oh, my lord, give her the living child! Don't kill him!" But the other woman said, "He shall be neither mine nor yours. Cut him in two."

Then the king said, "Give the living child to the woman who cried out for him, and do not kill him. She is his mother."

All Israel heard about the king's judgment, and they were in awe of him. For they saw that the wisdom of God was in him, to do justice.

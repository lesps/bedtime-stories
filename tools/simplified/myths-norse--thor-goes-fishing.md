After his trip to Utgard, Thor could not stop thinking about that grey cat that he could not lift, the one that was really the Midgard Serpent. The Midgard Serpent was the biggest snake there ever was. It lay at the bottom of the sea, all the way around the world, with its tail in its mouth. Thor wanted another chance at it.

So one day, Thor disguised himself as a young man and walked all the way to the house of a giant named Hymir, who lived by the sea. He spent the night there. In the morning he saw Hymir getting his boat ready to go fishing.

"Can I come too?" asked Thor.

Hymir looked down at him. "A little fellow like you?" he scoffed. "You'll be no use at all. And you'll freeze to death, the way I go far out and stay out for so long."

"I'll row as far out as you like," said Thor. "We'll see which of us wants to go home first." He was so cross that he almost bopped the giant on the head with his hammer then and there. But he was saving his strength for something else.

"What shall we use for bait?" asked Thor.

"Find your own bait," said Hymir.

So Thor went to Hymir's herd of cattle, picked out the biggest bull, and took its head for his bait. Then he got into the boat.

Thor sat at the back with two oars and rowed. He rowed so hard and so fast that Hymir, at the front, was amazed at how quickly the boat flew over the water.

"This is where I always fish for flounder," said Hymir. "Stop here."

"Let's go a bit farther," said Thor, and he kept on rowing.

"Stop!" cried Hymir after a while. "If we go any farther, we'll be in danger from the Midgard Serpent!"

But Thor kept rowing, and rowing, a long way farther. Then at last he put down the oars. He took out a very strong fishing line with a very strong hook, put the bull's head on the hook and threw it into the sea. Down, down, down it went, all the way to the bottom.

Down there, the Midgard Serpent saw the tasty bull's head, and gulped it down. The hook stuck fast in its mouth.

The serpent pulled. It pulled so hard that Thor was jerked forward and had to brace his hands against the side of the boat. Then Thor got angry, and he pulled back with all his godly strength. He pulled so hard that his feet went right through the bottom of the boat, and he stood on the floor of the sea! And with his hands he hauled the great serpent up out of the water, right up to the side of the boat.

What a sight it was! Thor glared at the serpent with his fiery eyes, and the serpent glared back at Thor and spat poison at him.

When Hymir saw the monster, he went pale and trembled all over. And the water was pouring into the boat through the hole Thor had made. Just as Thor raised his hammer to strike, Hymir grabbed his knife and cut the line. Splash! The serpent sank back down into the sea.

Thor threw his hammer after it. Some say he knocked the serpent's head off at the bottom of the sea. But most people are sure that the Midgard Serpent is still alive, lying around the world under the ocean.

Thor was so cross with Hymir that he gave him a whack on the ear that knocked him head over heels into the sea. Then Thor waded back to the shore with great big steps.

And the Midgard Serpent and Thor are enemies to this day. The old stories say they will meet one more time, at the very end of the world.

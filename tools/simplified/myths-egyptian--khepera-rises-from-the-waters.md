---
title: Khepera Rises from the Waters
adaptedFrom: budge-legends--the-creation
order: 1
flags: none
namesNote: Budge's text calls the creator Neb-er-tcher, "the Lord to the uttermost limit," who took the form of Khepera, the god of the rising sun; Nu is the dark ocean (also called Nun), and Seb is Geb, the earth.
---

In the very beginning, there was no sky, and no earth. There were no plants, and no animals, and no people. There was only Nu, a dark, endless ocean of still water, where nothing moved.

Then, out of the dark water, the great god came into being, all by himself. He took the form of Khepera, the god who would one day roll the sun up into the sky every morning.

But there was nowhere for Khepera to stand. There was only water. So he spoke a magic word inside his heart, and he made a place to stand. Then, in his heart, he planned everything that would ever be. And everything that he thought of came into being, one thing after another.

At first Khepera was all alone. There was nobody to help him. So he sent out from himself Shu, the god of the air. And he spat, and out came Tefnut, the goddess of moisture. Now there were three gods instead of one.

Shu and Tefnut went away from him, out into the dark ocean of Nu, and his Eye was hidden away behind them. For a long, long time they were gone. But at last Shu and Tefnut came rising up out of the waters, and brought his Eye back to him.

Then Khepera gathered himself together, and he wept. And where his tears fell, men and women sprang up. That was how the first people came to be.

But while his Eye had been away, Khepera had made another eye to take its place. When his first Eye came back and saw the new one, it was angry. So Khepera gave it a place of great honour, on the front of his face, where it shines out over the whole earth. And the two eyes became the sun and the moon.

Then Khepera made all the creeping things, and every other kind of living thing.

And Shu and Tefnut had children of their own: Geb, the god of the earth, and Nut, the goddess of the sky. And Geb and Nut had five children, born one after another: Osiris, Horus the Elder, Set, Isis and Nephthys. And their children filled the world.

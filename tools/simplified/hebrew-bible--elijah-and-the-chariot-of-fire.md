The time came when the Lord was going to take Elijah up to heaven in a whirlwind. Elijah and Elisha were walking together from Gilgal.

"Stay here, please," Elijah said to Elisha. "The Lord has sent me on to Beth-el."

"As the Lord lives, and as you live," said Elisha, "I will not leave you." So they went down to Beth-el together.

At Beth-el, the followers of the prophets came out to Elisha. "Do you know that today the Lord is going to take your master away from you?" they asked.

"Yes, I know," said Elisha. "Don't talk about it."

"Stay here, please, Elisha," said Elijah. "The Lord has sent me on to Jericho."

"As the Lord lives, and as you live," said Elisha, "I will not leave you." So they went to Jericho.

At Jericho, the followers of the prophets came to Elisha. "Do you know that today the Lord is going to take your master away from you?"

"Yes, I know," said Elisha. "Don't talk about it."

"Stay here, please," said Elijah. "The Lord has sent me on to the river Jordan."

"As the Lord lives, and as you live," said Elisha, "I will not leave you." And the two of them went on together.

Fifty of the followers of the prophets went too, and stood watching from far off, as the two of them stood beside the Jordan. Elijah took off his cloak, and rolled it up, and struck the water with it. And the water parted to the left and to the right, so that the two of them crossed over on dry ground.

When they had crossed, Elijah said to Elisha, "Ask me for whatever you want me to do for you, before I am taken away from you."

"Please," said Elisha, "let me have a double share of your spirit."

"You have asked a hard thing," said Elijah. "But if you see me when I am taken from you, it will be yours. If you don't, it won't."

As they walked on, still talking, suddenly a chariot of fire and horses of fire came between them, and parted them. And Elijah went up to heaven in a whirlwind.

Elisha saw it, and he cried out, "My father, my father! The chariots of Israel and its horsemen!" And then he saw him no more. Elisha took hold of his own clothes and tore them in two.

Then he picked up Elijah's cloak, which had fallen from him, and went back and stood on the bank of the Jordan. He struck the water with Elijah's cloak, and said, "Where is the Lord, the God of Elijah?" And the water parted to the left and to the right, and Elisha crossed over.

When the followers of the prophets at Jericho saw him from far off, they said, "The spirit of Elijah rests on Elisha." And they came to meet him, and bowed down to the ground before him.

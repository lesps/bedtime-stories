Jacob left Beer-sheba, and set out for the land of Haran.

He came to a certain place, and stopped there for the night, because the sun had set. He took one of the stones of that place, and put it under his head, and lay down to sleep.

And he dreamed. In his dream there was a ladder standing on the earth, and its top reached up to heaven. And the angels of God were going up and coming down on it.

And there was the Lord, standing beside him. "I am the Lord," He said, "the God of Abraham your grandfather, and the God of Isaac. The land you are lying on, I will give to you and to your children after you. Your children's children will be as many as the specks of dust on the earth, and they will spread out to the west and the east, to the north and the south. And through you and your children, all the families of the earth will be blessed.

"Look, I am with you. I will keep you safe wherever you go, and I will bring you back to this land. I will not leave you until I have done everything I have promised you."

Jacob woke up from his sleep. "Surely the Lord is in this place," he said, "and I did not know it!" And he was filled with awe. "How awesome this place is!" he said. "This is none other than the house of God, and this is the gate of heaven."

Early in the morning, Jacob got up. He took the stone he had put under his head, and stood it up as a pillar, and poured oil on top of it. And he called that place Beth-el, which means the house of God. Before that, the town there had been called Luz.

Then Jacob made a promise. "If God will be with me," he said, "and keep me safe on this journey, and give me bread to eat and clothes to wear, so that I come back to my father's house in peace, then the Lord shall be my God. And this stone that I have set up as a pillar shall be God's house. And of everything You give me, I will give a tenth back to You."

namesNote: From the Mahabharata. Markandeya is a sage who has lived through many ages. The child is Narayana, another name of Vishnu, and Markandeya tells the Pandavas that he was Krishna.

The sage Markandeya has lived through many ages of the world, and he told the Pandava princes what he saw at the end of one of them.

At the end of the age, the whole world was destroyed. Everything became one great sea of water. There were no gods and no demons, no people, no trees and no beasts; even the sky was gone. "Only I was left," said Markandeya, "and I wandered over the endless water, sad and lonely, because there was no one else anywhere. I was so tired, but there was nowhere to rest.

"Then, after a long time, I saw a huge banyan tree in the middle of the water. On one of its long branches, on a soft bed, sat a little boy. His face was as lovely as a lotus or the moon, and his eyes were as large as lotus petals. I was amazed. 'How can this little boy be sitting here alone, when the whole world has been destroyed?' I wondered. Even though I know the past and the present and the future, I could not tell who he was.

"Then the boy spoke to me, in a voice that was sweet to hear. 'Markandeya,' he said, 'I know you are tired and want to rest. Come inside my body and rest there, as long as you like. I am pleased with you.' And then the boy opened his mouth, and before I could move, I found myself inside it.

"And inside the boy, I saw the whole world! I saw cities and kingdoms. I saw the rivers Ganga and Yamuna and Saraswati and Godavari and Kaveri, and many more. I saw the ocean full of fish and sharks, and the sky with the sun and the moon. I saw forests and woods, and the Himalayas and the golden mountain Meru, and all the other mountains of the earth. I saw lions and tigers and boars and every other animal. I saw the gods, with Indra at their head, and spirits and serpents and birds, and even the demons. Everything that moves and everything that does not move on the earth, I saw inside the body of that child.

"For hundreds of years I wandered inside him, living on fruit, travelling all over the world that was there, and I never came to the end of his body. At last I prayed to him with all my heart. And then a gust of wind blew me out of his mouth, and there I was again, beside the banyan tree. And there sat the same little boy on the branch, who had swallowed the whole universe.

"He smiled at me. 'Markandeya,' he said, 'you must be tired from living inside my body.' And at that moment I could see the truth more clearly than ever before. I bowed down at his feet, and said, 'I want to know who you are. Why do you stay here, in the shape of a little boy, with the whole universe inside you?'

"And he told me. 'I am Narayana,' he said, 'and I make all things and unmake them. When the world is ended, I sleep here in the shape of a child, until Brahma, the creator, wakes up. Then I make the world again.'

"And then that wonderful Being vanished. And I saw the whole wide world come to life again, as it is now."

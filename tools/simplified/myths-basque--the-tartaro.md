---
title: The Tartaro, the One-Eyed Giant

namesNote: In Basque, "Heben nuk!" means "You've got me here!"
---

Long ago, in the Basque Country, there lived the Tartaro. He was an enormous giant, with only one eye, right in the middle of his forehead.

The Tartaro was a shepherd and a hunter. But the animals he hunted were people. Every day he ate a whole sheep. Then he had a snooze. And when he woke up, he ate anyone who had been unlucky enough to fall into his hands.

He lived in a huge barn, with thick walls, a high roof, and a strong door that only he knew how to open. And his mother, an old witch, lived in a little hut made of turf, in a corner of the garden.

One day, a strong young man was caught in the Tartaro's traps, and the Tartaro carried him off to his barn. The young man watched the giant eat a whole sheep. He knew that the Tartaro always had a snooze after his dinner, and that after that, it would be his turn.

"I must do something," he said to himself.

As soon as the Tartaro began to snore, the young man put the iron roasting spit into the fire, and heated it until it was red-hot. Then he plunged it into the giant's one eye.

The Tartaro leapt up with a roar, and ran about looking for him. But now he could not see a thing, and he could not find the young man anywhere.

"You won't escape!" shouted the giant. "Hide all you like. Only I know the secret of how to open this door!"

The Tartaro opened the door halfway, and let his sheep out one by one between his legs. Then the young man had an idea. He took the big bell off the ram's neck and hung it round his own neck. He threw the skin of the sheep that the giant had eaten over his back. And he crawled to the door on his hands and knees, ringing the bell, just like a sheep.

The Tartaro felt him with his hands as he went past, and he knew it was a trick. He grabbed hold of the sheepskin. But the young man wriggled out of the skin, dived between the giant's legs, and ran away.

Outside, he met the Tartaro's mother, the old witch. "Oh, you lucky young man," she said. "You have escaped from that cruel giant. Here, take this ring, to remember your escape."

The young man took the ring and put it on his finger. At once the ring began to shout, "Heben nuk! Heben nuk!" which means, "You've got me here! You've got me here!"

The blind Tartaro heard the ring shouting, and followed the sound. The young man ran and ran, but wherever he went, the ring kept shouting, and the giant kept coming closer. He tried to pull the ring off, but it would not come off.

The Tartaro was just about to catch him. So the young man took out his knife, and cut off his own finger, ring and all, and threw it far away into a deep bog.

The ring went on shouting, "Heben nuk! Heben nuk!" from the middle of the bog. And the Tartaro, following the sound, ran straight into the bog after it, and sank, and was drowned.

And the young man got safely away.

title: Mujo and the Mountain Fairies

Long ago, in the mountains of the north, there was a poor boy called Mujo. He had no money and no land of his own, so he worked for other people, looking after their cows up in the high mountain pastures.

One day, when Mujo was out on the mountain with the cows, he heard crying. He followed the sound, and there, under the hot sun, he found two little cradles, and in them two tiny babies, crying and crying. There was nobody else around.

Mujo felt sorry for them. He cut some leafy branches and made a shade over the cradles, to keep the hot sun off the babies. Then he rocked the cradles gently, until the babies stopped crying and fell asleep. And he sat beside them and kept watch until the evening.

What Mujo didn't know was that these were the babies of the zanas, the fairies of the mountains.

That evening, the zanas came back to their babies. When they saw how well they had been looked after, they were very pleased. "Who looked after our children?" they asked. "Come out, so that we can reward you!"

Mujo came out, and the zanas said, "You have been kind to our children, so we will give you whatever you want. Would you like gold and riches? Or would you like to be wise? Or would you like to be the strongest hero in all the land?"

Mujo thought for a moment. Then he said, "I would like to be strong."

So the zanas gave Mujo their fairy milk to drink. When he had drunk it once, they said, "Now try to lift that great rock." Mujo tried, and the rock moved, but only a little. So he drank a second time, and now he could lift the rock up off the ground. Then he drank a third time, and he lifted the great rock high above his head, and threw it far away across the mountain.

"Now you are strong," said the zanas.

So Mujo went home, and from that day on, he was the strongest hero in all the land. He and his brother Halil had many adventures, and the people sang songs about them. And whenever Mujo was in trouble, the zanas of the mountains came to help him.

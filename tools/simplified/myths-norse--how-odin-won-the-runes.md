title: How Odin Won the Runes

Odin, the father of the gods, always wanted to know more. He already knew a great deal. But there was a secret that even he did not know: the secret of the runes.

Runes are letters, the old letters of the North. But they are not just letters for writing words. Long ago, people believed runes were full of magic. Whoever knew the runes could carve them, and read them, and use them to do wonderful things.

To learn the runes, Odin did something very hard. He went to a tall tree, a tree so old and so great that no one knows where its roots go. And there, in the wind, he hung from the tree for nine whole nights, wounded by a spear, giving himself to himself, as a gift to Odin. The wind rocked the tree back and forth. No one gave him any bread. No one gave him a single sip of water.

On the ninth night, Odin peered down, down into the deep. And there, far below, he saw the runes. With a great cry he reached down and grabbed them up. And then he fell from the tree.

He had won the runes.

After that, Odin learned nine powerful songs from a wise giant, his own mother's brother. And he got a drink of the most precious mead in the world, the mead that makes people wise and makes them poets. And then, Odin said, "I began to grow. I began to know many things. One word led me to another word, and one deed led me to another deed."

Then Odin carved the runes, and shared them out. He gave them to the gods, and the elves, and the dwarfs, and even the giants. Some he carved himself.

And with the runes came magic songs, and Odin knew them all. He told about them himself. "I know songs," he said, "that no king's wife knows, and no man's son.

"The first song is called Help. It will help you in your troubles and your sorrows.

"The second song is for those who want to be healers.

"With the third song, I can make my enemies' swords so blunt that they cannot cut.

"With the fourth, if someone puts chains on my arms and legs, I sing, and the chains fall off, and I can walk away.

"With the fifth, if I see an arrow flying toward me in battle, I can stop it in the air, if only I can see it.

"With the sixth, if someone wishes to harm me, the harm goes back to them instead.

"With the seventh, if I see a house burning with people inside, I can sing, and the fire will not burn them.

"With the eighth, if there is hatred between people, I can quickly make peace.

"With the ninth, if my ship is in danger, I can calm the wind and lull the waves to sleep.

"With the tenth, if I see witches flying through the air at night, I can send them away, all confused.

"With the eleventh, I can sing over my friends as they go into battle, and they will come home safe.

"And there are more songs still," said Odin. "I know one that I have never taught to anyone, and never will, for a secret is best when only one person knows it."

So that is how Odin won the runes, by being brave and patient, and paying a high price. And ever since, people have carved runes on stones and swords and bones, all across the North. You can still see them today, on old rune stones standing in the fields.
